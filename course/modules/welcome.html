What you'll learn

This course introduces SAP ERP, S/4HANA, and major SAP cloud products (SuccessFactors, Ariba, Concur, CX, IBP, SAC, BTP, Signavio, and more). Each chapter covers one module or product with its purpose, features, data plane & control plane, deployment, integration, key tables or entities, and a short quiz.

Foundation first. Module 1 establishes shared concepts (data plane, control plane, deployment, integration) used across all SAP solutions. You'll refer back to it as you move through each module.

Course structure

Foundation & Core ERP (Modules 1–12)
Foundation concepts, SAP Overview, FI, CO, MM, SD, PP, QM, PM, PS, HCM, WM/EWM.
SAP Cloud & Products (Modules 13–23)
SuccessFactors, Fieldglass, Ariba, Concur, CX, TM, IBP, SAC, Datasphere, BTP, Signavio.

ABAP, BW/BI, Basis, Security, Fiori, Integration (PI/CPI), S/4HANA Overview, Cross-Module Best Practices.
