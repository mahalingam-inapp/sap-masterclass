Module 14: SAP Fieldglass

For beginners: Fieldglass is SAP’s cloud solution for managing the external workforce—temporary staff, contractors, and project-based services (SOW). You do not hire these people as employees; you engage them through agencies or statements of work. Fieldglass handles the full flow: request a contingent worker or project, onboard them, record time and expenses, approve and pay invoices, and stay compliant. It is SaaS only and often connects to SuccessFactors (for worker/org data), Ariba (procurement and invoicing), and S/4HANA (cost center and G/L posting). Key terms: VMS (Vendor Management System), contingent labor, SOW (Statement of Work).

SAP Fieldglass is a VMS for the external workforce. It integrates with SuccessFactors, Ariba, and S/4HANA.

Purpose and scope

Fieldglass helps centralize and control spending on contingent labor and SOW services. Key areas: Contingent workforce: Request a worker → staffing vendor supplies candidate → worker onboarded in Fieldglass → timesheets and expense → invoice and payment. SOW (Statement of Work): Project-based engagements (e.g. consulting project with deliverables and milestones); request, approval, and invoice against milestones or deliverables. Compliance (worker classification, rate cards, co-employment risk) and analytics (spend by vendor, department) are built in. Integration with Ariba enables PO and invoice flow; integration with S/4HANA posts cost to the right cost center and G/L.

Features in detail

  • Requisition and request: Manager requests contingent worker (job title, rate, duration) or SOW project (scope, milestones). Approval workflow. Request goes to staffing vendor (contingent) or to supplier (SOW).
  • Worker and assignment: Vendor submits candidate; hiring manager approves. Worker is onboarded in Fieldglass (profile, rate, assignment). For SOW: supplier and project are set up with deliverables.
  • Time and expense: Contingent workers submit timesheets (hours by week); optional expense reports. Manager approves. Data feeds invoicing.
  • Invoicing: Vendor (or system) generates invoice from approved time and expense or from SOW milestones. Invoice can be sent to Ariba for matching and payment, or to S/4HANA for AP.
  • Compliance and analytics: Rate cards, worker classification (e.g. 1099 vs W-2), visibility into spend by vendor and department.

Data plane and control plane

Data plane: Tenant entities: Program, Request, Worker, Assignment, Timesheet, Expense, Invoice, SOW project. No customer DB; access via UIs and APIs. Control plane: Fieldglass Admin (tenant settings, workflows, rate cards, integration); IAS for SSO. See Module 1.

Deployment and integration

Deployment: SaaS only; tenant per customer (or per region). Integration: SuccessFactors (worker/employee data); Ariba (catalog, PO, invoice); S/4HANA (cost center, G/L, AP); Concur (expense where used); CPI for sync and APIs (Module 29).

Example: Contingent worker from request to invoice

IT needs a contractor for 3 months at 75 USD/hour.

  1. Requisition: Manager creates request in Fieldglass: role “Java Developer,” 40 h/week, 12 weeks, rate 75 USD/h, cost center 2000. Request is approved and sent to preferred staffing vendor.
  2. Worker and assignment: Vendor submits candidate “Jane Doe.” Manager approves. Jane is onboarded in Fieldglass: assignment start/end, rate, cost center. Jane gets access to submit time.
  3. Time and invoice: Each week Jane submits timesheet (40 h). Manager approves. At month end, vendor invoice is generated from approved time (e.g. 160 h × 75 = 12,000 USD). Invoice is sent to Ariba or S/4HANA; payment is made. Cost is allocated to cost center 2000 in S/4HANA.

Result: Contingent spend is visible, approved, and posted to the correct cost center.

Key concepts

Contingent vs SOW: Contingent = time-and-materials (hours × rate); SOW = project-based (deliverables or milestones). VMS: Vendor Management System—one place to manage multiple staffing vendors and rates. Integration with Ariba: Fieldglass can create POs and send invoices to Ariba for procurement and payment. S/4HANA: Cost center and G/L for posting; optional worker replication from SuccessFactors.
