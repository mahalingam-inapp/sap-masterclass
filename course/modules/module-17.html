Module 17: SAP Customer Experience (CX)

For beginners: SAP CX (formerly C/4HANA) is SAP’s cloud CRM and customer-facing suite. It includes Sales Cloud (opportunities, quotes, orders), Service Cloud (support tickets, field service), Marketing Cloud (campaigns, leads), and Commerce Cloud (B2B/B2C storefronts). Companies use CX for the “front office” (sales, marketing, service, e-commerce) while keeping S/4HANA as the system of record for fulfillment, product master, pricing, and finance. Orders created in CX can be sent to S/4HANA for delivery and billing; product and price can flow from S/4HANA to CX. Integration is via APIs and often SAP BTP (CPI, Event Mesh). Key terms: CRM, Sales/Service/Marketing/Commerce Cloud, CDP (unified customer data).

SAP CX covers the full customer journey in the cloud. Integration with S/4HANA is via APIs and BTP.

Purpose and scope

CX covers the full customer journey: Marketing Cloud: Campaigns, lead management, and (with CDP) unified customer data. Sales Cloud: Opportunities, quotes, and (with Revenue Cloud) subscription and billing. Service Cloud: Cases/tickets, knowledge base, field service. Commerce Cloud: B2B and B2C storefronts; cart and checkout can create orders in S/4HANA or in Commerce. CDP: Single view of customer from many sources. Revenue Cloud: Subscription billing and revenue recognition. Orders and pricing can be authored in CX and replicated to S/4HANA for fulfillment and FI; or S/4HANA can be the source of product and price and CX the front-end.

Features in detail

  • Sales Cloud: Accounts and contacts; opportunities and pipeline; quotes and orders. Integration: order can create sales order in S/4HANA (SD); product and price from S/4HANA or CX.
  • Service Cloud: Service tickets (cases); knowledge base and self-service; field service (scheduling, mobile). Warranty and installed base can come from S/4HANA.
  • Marketing Cloud: Campaigns, segments, and lead scoring; integration with CDP for customer data. Leads can flow to Sales Cloud.
  • Commerce Cloud: Catalog, cart, checkout; B2B (e.g. customer-specific pricing, contract) and B2C. Order can be sent to S/4HANA for delivery and billing.
  • Revenue Cloud: Subscription and usage-based billing; revenue recognition rules. Can integrate with S/4HANA FI for revenue posting.

Data plane and control plane

Data plane: Tenant entities: Account, Contact, Opportunity, Quote, Order, Case (ticket), Product, etc. No customer DB; UIs and APIs. Sync to/from S/4HANA (order, product, customer, pricing) via CPI or direct APIs. Control plane: Product-specific admin (Sales, Service, Marketing, Commerce); SAP Build for custom UIs; Integration Suite (CPI) and IAS. See Module 1.

Deployment and integration

Deployment: SaaS only; multiple products (Sales, Service, Marketing, Commerce) can be licensed together. Integration: S/4HANA: order creation (CX → SD), product and pricing (S/4HANA → CX or bidirectional), customer (sync); FI/AR (billing, revenue). BTP (CPI, API Management, Event Mesh); IAS for SSO (Module 22, 29).

Example: Quote in Sales Cloud to order in S/4HANA

Sales rep works in Sales Cloud; order is fulfilled in S/4HANA.

  1. Opportunity and quote (Sales Cloud): Rep has opportunity for customer “Acme.” Creates quote: 2× Product A @ 100 EUR, 1× Product B @ 200 EUR. Quote total 400 EUR. Customer accepts.
  2. Order creation: Rep converts quote to order in Sales Cloud. Integration (CPI or API) creates sales order in S/4HANA: customer Acme, items Product A (2), Product B (1), price from quote. Order number in S/4HANA is returned and stored in CX.
  3. Fulfillment (S/4HANA): Delivery (VL01N) and billing (VF01) happen in S/4HANA. Revenue and AR post to FI. Optional: status or invoice data can be sent back to CX for visibility.

Result: Sell in CX; fulfill and revenue in S/4HANA.

Key concepts

CX vs SD: CX = cloud CRM and digital experience; SD = ERP order, delivery, billing. Typical pattern: CX for selling and service; S/4HANA for order execution and FI. Integration patterns: Order from CX to S/4HANA (create sales order); product and price from S/4HANA to CX; customer sync both ways. BTP: CPI for orchestration; API Management for APIs; Event Mesh for events (Module 22, 29).

📝 Module Quiz

Q1: SAP CX includes:

  • A) Only MM
  • B) Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Marketing Cloud, Commerce Cloud
  • C) Only FI
  • D) Only Basis
Correct: B
CX = Customer Experience (sales, service, marketing, commerce).

Q2: Service Cloud is for:

  • A) Only procurement
  • B) Service tickets, knowledge base, field service
  • C) Only production
  • D) Only warehouse
Correct: B
Service Cloud = customer service and support.

Q3: CX integrates with S/4HANA for:

  • A) Only QM
  • B) Only PM
  • C) Orders, pricing, product master, FI/AR
  • D) Only PS
Correct: C
Orders, pricing, product, billing/revenue flow to S/4HANA.

Q4: Where is the sales order typically created when using Sales Cloud with S/4HANA?

  • A) Only in FI
  • B) In S/4HANA (SD), triggered by order creation from Sales Cloud via API/CPI
  • C) Only in MM
  • D) Only in PP
Correct: B
Sales Cloud sends order data to S/4HANA; SD creates the sales order for fulfillment.
