Module 11: Human Capital Management (HCM)

For beginners: Human Capital Management (HCM) in SAP ERP is the on-premise HR solution: employee data, organization structure, time recording, and payroll. Employee data is stored in infotypes—time-dependent records such as “basic pay” or “address” with a validity period, so you keep history. Organizational Management defines the structure: org units, positions, jobs, and which person holds which position. Time management covers clock-in/out, leave, and absences. Payroll calculates gross and net pay and can post salary costs to FI. Many companies are moving to cloud HCM (SuccessFactors)—covered in Modules 13–14—but core ERP HCM is still widely used or integrated with cloud.

SAP HCM in ERP covers personnel administration, organizational management, time management, and payroll. Cloud HCM (SuccessFactors, Fieldglass) is covered in Modules 13 and 14; this module focuses on the core ERP HCM component.

Purpose and scope

HCM supports the employee lifecycle: hiring, personnel data (address, contract, cost center assignment), organization structure (positions, jobs, org units), time recording (attendances, absences, leave), and payroll (salary, tax, deductions, bank transfer). Results can be posted to FI (salary expense, payables). Training (LSO), recruitment (RCM), and talent modules exist in ECC/S/4HANA; SAP’s strategic direction is cloud (SuccessFactors) for many of these areas.

In plain terms: An infotype is a type of personnel record—e.g. “0002 Personal Data” or “0008 Basic Pay.” Each infotype has a validity period (from–to date), so when someone’s salary changes, you create a new record and the old one stays for history. The organization structure (org unit → position → person) is used for reporting (“who reports to whom”) and often for cost center assignment and authorizations.

Features in detail

  • Personnel Administration (PA): Infotypes store employee data: 0001 (org assignment), 0002 (personal data), 0006 (address), 0007 (planned working time), 0008 (basic pay), etc. Each infotype has validity period; history is kept. PA30 (maintain), PA20 (display).
  • Organizational Management (OM): Object types: org unit, position, job, person. Structure: company → org unit → position (held by person). Used for reporting, authorization, and cost center assignment. PP01 (org structure), PPOME (org chart).
  • Time Management (PT): Time events (clock in/out), attendances, absences (leave, sickness). Leave requests and approval; quota (annual leave). Integration with payroll: absences can deduct pay or trigger allowances.
  • Payroll: Payroll run (country-specific: e.g. PYxx for Germany, US, etc.) reads infotypes (pay, tax, deductions) and time data, calculates gross and net, and creates payroll results. Posting to FI: salary expense, employee payables, tax and social. Payslips and bank transfer (payment run).

Data plane and control plane

Data plane: Infotypes (e.g. PA0001, PA0002) and cluster tables (payroll results). Data is time-dependent (validity period). Control plane: Personnel area, employee group/subgroup, pay scale and wage types, payroll schema and customizing (SPRO). See Module 1.

Deployment and integration

Deployment: On‑premise (ECC, S/4HANA). S/4HANA Cloud can use SuccessFactors for HCM (Module 13) and keep payroll on-premise or use SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central Payroll. Integration: Postings to FI (salary, tax); optional integration with SuccessFactors (Employee Central), Concur (expenses), and external payroll services (Module 29).

Example: New hire and first payroll

Company hires a new employee in cost center 1000, position 10001234.

  1. Create personnel number and org assignment (PA30): Create employee, infotype 0001 (org assignment): personnel area 1000, employee group/subgroup, cost center 1000, position 10001234. Infotype 0002 (name, DOB), 0006 (address), 0008 (basic pay: 50,000 EUR/year). Valid from hire date.
  2. Time recording: Employee records time (or manager enters). Absences (e.g. leave) are recorded. Payroll reads this for the period.
  3. Payroll run (e.g. PC00_M99_CALC): Payroll for month: gross from infotype 0008 and time data; tax and deductions calculated; net pay and employer contributions. Results stored in cluster. Posting run: Post to FI: Dr Salary expense (cost center 1000), Cr Employee payables, Cr Tax payables, etc.

Result: Employee in org structure; first salary expense and liabilities in FI.
