Module 15: SAP Ariba

For beginners: Ariba is SAP’s cloud procurement solution. It covers both strategic sourcing (finding and selecting suppliers: eRFx, auctions) and procure-to-pay (P2P): creating requisitions, purchase orders, and processing supplier invoices in the cloud. The Ariba Network is a network of buyers and suppliers—when you send a PO or receive an invoice, it can flow electronically through the network. Companies often integrate Ariba with S/4HANA: for example, a PO created in Ariba is replicated to S/4HANA for goods receipt and FI posting, or the invoice is sent from Ariba to S/4HANA via CPI. Key terms: P2P, Ariba Network, CPI (integration).

SAP Ariba covers sourcing and P2P in the cloud. Integration with S/4HANA is typically via CPI or CIG.

Purpose and scope

Ariba addresses the full procurement cycle in the cloud: Sourcing: eRFQ, eRFP, reverse auctions to select suppliers and negotiate terms. Contract management: Author and manage contracts; link to catalog and PO. Procure-to-pay: Catalog or free-text requisition → approval → PO → (optional GR in S/4HANA) → supplier sends invoice via Ariba Network → invoice matched and paid. Supplier lifecycle: onboarding, qualification, risk. Spend analytics and compliance (policy, delegation) are part of the suite. Many customers use Ariba for upstream (sourcing, contracts) and either Ariba or S/4HANA for downstream (PO, invoice, payment).

Features in detail

  • Strategic sourcing: Create sourcing events (eRFQ, eRFP); suppliers respond; compare and award. Reverse auctions for competitive bidding. Results feed contracts.
  • Contracts: Contract authoring, templates, approval. Contract release orders (CRO) can create POs against contract. Link to catalog.
  • Requisition and PO: User shops from catalog or creates free-text req; approval workflow; PO is created and sent to supplier via Ariba Network. PO can replicate to S/4HANA (MM) for GR and tracking.
  • Ariba Network: Suppliers receive POs and send invoices electronically. Invoices are matched to PO (and optionally GR in S/4HANA). Payment can be via Ariba or pushed to S/4HANA for payment run.
  • Supplier management: Supplier onboarding, qualification, risk and performance. Central supplier master in the cloud.

Data plane and control plane

Data plane: Tenant data: catalog, requisition, PO, contract, invoice, supplier. No customer DB; UIs and APIs. Replication to/from S/4HANA (PO, invoice, vendor) via CPI/CIG. Control plane: Ariba Admin (tenant, workflows, approval, integration settings); CIG or CPI for connection to S/4HANA. See Module 1.

Deployment and integration

Deployment: SaaS only; Ariba Network is a shared network (buyers and suppliers). Integration: S/4HANA: PO from Ariba → S/4HANA for GR and FI; invoice from Ariba Network → S/4HANA for MIRO and AP; or PO created in S/4HANA and sent to Ariba for supplier. Catalog and vendor sync. CPI or CIG (Module 29) for replication.

Example: Procure from Ariba catalog to S/4HANA FI

Employee needs office supplies; company uses Ariba for buying and S/4HANA for GR and payment.

  1. Requisition (Ariba): User creates requisition from catalog (e.g. “Printer paper, 10 reams”) or free-text. Submits for approval. Approver approves.
  2. PO (Ariba): PO is created and sent to supplier via Ariba Network. PO is replicated to S/4HANA (e.g. via CIG): creates purchase order in MM (or reference document). Plant and cost center from requisition.
  3. GR and invoice (S/4HANA + Ariba): Warehouse posts goods receipt in S/4HANA (MIGO) against the replicated PO. Supplier submits invoice in Ariba Network; invoice is sent to S/4HANA. MIRO matches invoice to PO and GR; FI document is created (AP). Payment run in S/4HANA pays the vendor.

Result: Buying in Ariba; fulfillment and payment in S/4HANA; one end-to-end P2P flow.

Key concepts

P2P: Procure-to-pay = requisition → PO → receive → invoice → pay. Ariba Network: Suppliers connect once and receive POs from many buyers; send invoices electronically. Integration scenarios: Ariba-led (buy in Ariba, replicate PO to S/4HANA for GR/FI); S/4HANA-led (create PO in S/4HANA, send to Ariba for supplier); hybrid. CIG: Cloud Integration Gateway—prebuilt connectors for Ariba–S/4HANA. CPI can replace or extend CIG (Module 29).
