Module 19: SAP Integrated Business Planning (IBP)

For beginners: IBP is SAP’s cloud planning suite for demand and supply. It answers questions like “how much will we sell?” (demand planning), “how much do we need to make or buy?” (supply planning), and “how do we balance demand and supply?” (Sales & Operations Planning, S&OP). IBP reads data from S/4HANA (and other sources)—demand, inventory, master data—and can write planned orders or forecasts back to S/4HANA. It is SaaS only and is used for collaborative, advanced planning across sales, supply chain, and finance. Key terms: demand planning, supply planning, S&OP, IBP.

SAP IBP covers demand planning, supply planning, inventory optimization, and S&OP. It integrates with S/4HANA for data and write-back.

Purpose and scope

IBP supports integrated planning: Demand: Forecast at product/location level; statistical forecasting and consensus demand. Supply: Supply planning and capacity; planned orders; allocation. Inventory: Safety stock and inventory optimization. Response: What-if and supply control. S&OP: Demand vs supply reconciliation; financial impact; scenario comparison. Data is loaded from S/4HANA (and BW, files) via integration jobs; results can be written back to S/4HANA (e.g. planned independent requirements, planned orders) so that MRP in S/4HANA can consume IBP output.

Features in detail

  • IBP for demand: Demand planning with statistical models; baseline and consensus forecast; demand sensing (short-term). Key figures: demand, forecast, history.
  • IBP for supply: Supply planning: match supply to demand; capacity constraints; planned order generation. Can push planned orders to S/4HANA.
  • IBP for inventory: Safety stock and reorder point; inventory optimization by location and product.
  • IBP for response and supply control: Short-term adjustments; allocation; what-if scenarios.
  • S&OP: Demand vs supply balance; financial view; scenario planning and approval workflows.

Data plane and control plane

Data plane: Cloud planning models (key figures, dimensions: product, location, time). Data from S/4HANA (demand, supply, inventory, master) via integration. No customer DB in IBP; data lives in IBP cloud storage. Control plane: IBP admin (planning areas, key figures, integration jobs); CPI or IBP integration for S/4HANA. See Module 1.

Deployment and integration

Deployment: SaaS only. Integration: S/4HANA: demand (sales orders, requirements), supply (stock, planned orders), inventory, master data (material, location). Data flows via CPI or IBP connectors. Results (e.g. forecast, planned orders) can be written to S/4HANA. SAC (Module 20) can consume IBP data for analytics; IBP has its own analytics and planning UIs.

Example: Demand plan in IBP feeding S/4HANA

Planning team runs monthly demand planning in IBP; forecast is sent to S/4HANA for MRP.

  1. Data load (IBP): Integration job pulls from S/4HANA: historical demand (e.g. sales order history, past consumption), current stock, open orders. Master data (material, plant) is also loaded. Data appears in IBP planning views.
  2. Demand planning (IBP): Planners run statistical forecast (e.g. by product and plant). They adjust in consensus meetings. Final demand plan is saved (e.g. next 12 months by product/plant).
  3. Write-back to S/4HANA: IBP sends planned independent requirements (PIR) or forecast to S/4HANA. S/4HANA MRP (MD02/MD40) uses this as demand. MRP creates planned orders and purchase requisitions. Execution (PO, production) stays in S/4HANA.

Result: One demand plan in the cloud; S/4HANA executes supply.

Key concepts

Planning area / key figures: IBP organizes data in planning areas with dimensions (product, location, time) and key figures (demand, forecast, supply). Integration jobs: Scheduled or on-demand jobs to read from S/4HANA (and others) and write back. IBP vs MRP: IBP = cloud, advanced planning, S&OP; MRP in S/4HANA = execution-level material requirements. Many customers use IBP for tactical/strategic plan and S/4HANA MRP for execution.
