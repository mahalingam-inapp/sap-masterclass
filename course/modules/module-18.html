Module 18: SAP Transportation Management (TM)

For beginners: Transportation Management (TM) handles the freight side of logistics: once a delivery leaves the warehouse (SD/EWM), how do you plan the shipment, choose a carrier, tender the load, track it, and settle the freight cost? TM creates freight orders (the need to move goods) and shipments (the actual move with carrier and dates). It can run embedded in S/4HANA or as a standalone system. EWM covers the warehouse; TM covers the transport leg. Freight costs can be posted to FI. Key terms: freight order, shipment, tendering, carrier.

SAP TM covers freight planning, carrier selection, tendering, execution, and freight settlement. It is triggered by SD deliveries or EWM outbound.

Purpose and scope

TM answers: “How do we move goods from A to B at the right cost and time?” Key areas: Freight order: Represents the requirement to move goods (from delivery or manual). Planning: Load building (combine orders into shipments), route and carrier selection, optimization. Tendering: Send request to carrier; receive rate and confirmation. Execution: Shipment created; tracking and status events (e.g. picked up, in transit, delivered). Freight settlement: Carrier invoice; post freight cost to FI (cost object, G/L). Integration with SD (delivery document) and EWM (outbound order) provides the source of demand.

Features in detail

  • Freight order: Created from SD delivery or EWM outbound (or manually). Contains source/destination, weight/volume, requested dates. Can be planned and tendered.
  • Planning and optimization: Build loads (group freight orders); select carrier and service; optimize routes. Planning runs can suggest best combination.
  • Shipment: Execution object: one or more freight units (shipment legs). Carrier and planned dates. Status updates (events) during execution.
  • Tendering and carrier selection: Send tender to carrier (or use rate contract); carrier confirms; rate and capacity locked.
  • Freight settlement: After execution, carrier invoice is entered or received. Freight cost is posted to FI (cost center, G/L); can be allocated to delivery or order.

Data plane and control plane

Data plane: TM tables (e.g. /SCMTMS/*): freight order, freight unit, shipment, carrier, rate. Processing in application server. Control plane: TM customizing (SPRO): business partner (carrier), location, product (service), rate and settlement config. See Module 1.

Deployment and integration

Deployment: On‑premise and S/4HANA. TM can be embedded (same S/4HANA system) or standalone (separate TM system, integration via RFC/IDoc). Integration: SD (deliveries trigger freight demand); EWM (outbound delivery); FI (freight cost posting).

Example: Outbound delivery to freight order to settlement

SD delivery is created for a customer order; TM plans and executes the shipment.

  1. Trigger (SD/EWM): Outbound delivery 8000000123 is created (VL01N). TM receives the delivery: freight order is created (or updated) with source plant, ship-to address, weight, and requested delivery date.
  2. Planning: Planner runs planning (or it is automatic): freight order is assigned to a carrier (e.g. from rate contract). Shipment is created. Tender sent to carrier; carrier confirms. Shipment status: planned.
  3. Execution and settlement: Goods are handed to carrier; events are posted (picked up, in transit). On delivery, status “delivered.” Carrier sends invoice (e.g. 500 EUR). Freight settlement in TM: post to FI—Debit freight expense 500, Credit carrier payable 500. Cost can be allocated to the delivery or order.

Result: One shipment from delivery to delivery; freight cost in FI.

Key tables and concepts

TM uses namespace /SCMTMS/: freight order, freight unit, shipment, carrier, and related tables. Concepts: Freight order vs shipment; tendering; freight settlement document; integration with SD delivery and EWM outbound.
