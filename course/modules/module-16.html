Module 16: SAP Concur

For beginners: Concur is SAP’s travel and expense (T&E) cloud app. Employees book business travel (flights, hotels, car) and submit expense reports (meals, mileage, etc.); managers approve; the company pays and can post costs to the right cost center and G/L in S/4HANA. Concur is SaaS only—you use the web or mobile app, no on-premise install. Integration: employee and approver data often comes from SuccessFactors; approved expenses are sent to S/4HANA (or another ERP) so that FI gets the correct posting. Corporate card data can be fed into Concur so card transactions appear in the employee’s expense report. Key terms: T&E, expense report, approval workflow.

SAP Concur covers travel booking and expense reporting in the cloud. It integrates with S/4HANA and SuccessFactors.

Purpose and scope

Concur centralizes travel booking and expense management: Travel: Book air, hotel, car within policy; preferred vendors and negotiated rates. Expense: Create expense report (receipts, allocations to cost center/project); submit for approval; after approval, data can be sent to S/4HANA for posting (expense G/L, cost center, optional project). Reimbursement can be via payroll (employee reimbursement) or separate payment. Policy and audit: Policy rules (e.g. max meal amount, required receipt); audit trail and compliance reporting. Concur can also integrate with Fieldglass (expense for contingent workers) and with invoice (vendor invoices) in some deployments.

Features in detail

  • Travel booking: Search and book flights, hotels, car rental; policy checks (e.g. economy only); preferred suppliers. Trip itinerary and e-receipts.
  • Expense report: Employee creates report; adds expense entries (travel, meals, lodging, other) with receipt attachment. Allocates to cost center, project, or G/L. Submits for approval.
  • Approval workflow: Manager (or delegate) approves report. Multi-level approval and delegation rules. After approval, report can be exported to S/4HANA or payroll.
  • Integration to ERP: Concur connector (or CPI) sends approved expense data to S/4HANA: creates FI document (expense account, cost center, amount) and optionally clears against advance or triggers reimbursement. Employee master and approver can come from SuccessFactors or S/4HANA.
  • Corporate card: Card transactions flow into Concur; employee assigns to expense report and allocates. Reduces manual entry.

Data plane and control plane

Data plane: Tenant entities: User, Report, Expense (line items), Allocation (cost center, project). No customer DB; UIs and APIs. Posting data sent to S/4HANA (cost object, G/L). Control plane: Concur Admin (company settings, approval workflows, policy, integration); Concur Connector or CPI for S/4HANA. See Module 1.

Deployment and integration

Deployment: SaaS only; tenant per company. Integration: S/4HANA/FI (expense posting, cost center, G/L); SuccessFactors (employee, approver hierarchy); payroll (reimbursement); corporate card providers; CPI (Module 29) for custom flows.

Example: Expense report from Concur to S/4HANA

Employee travels for a project and submits an expense report.

  1. Expense report (Concur): Employee creates report “Q1 Trip – Berlin.” Adds entries: Flight 400 EUR, Hotel 3 nights 450 EUR, Meals 120 EUR. Attaches receipts. Allocates to cost center 2000 and project WBS P-001. Submits. Manager approves.
  2. Export to S/4HANA: Concur Connector (or CPI) sends approved report: total 970 EUR, cost center 2000, WBS P-001. System creates FI document in S/4HANA: Debit expense account(s) 970, Credit employee advance or payable 970; cost center and WBS from allocation. Or triggers reimbursement via payroll.
  3. Result in S/4HANA: Expense is on the books; cost center and project show the cost. Audit trail in Concur; financial trail in FI/CO.

Result: Policy-compliant expense; correct cost allocation in S/4HANA.

Key concepts

Travel vs expense: Travel = booking; expense = report and reimbursement. Often combined in one product. Allocation: Cost center, project (WBS), G/L—determines where cost posts in S/4HANA. Policy: Rules (receipt required over X, max per diem) enforced in Concur before or at approval. Integration: SuccessFactors for employee/approver; S/4HANA for posting; payroll for reimbursement.
