Module 13: SAP SuccessFactors

For beginners: SuccessFactors is SAP’s cloud HR (HCM) suite. Unlike on-premise HCM (Module 11), you do not run a database or server—SAP hosts everything. You get a tenant (your company’s instance) and use web apps for recruiting, employee data (Employee Central), goals and performance, compensation, learning, and succession. Employee and organizational data are the “source of truth” here; many companies replicate this data to S/4HANA (e.g. for cost center assignment or payroll) or to Concur, Ariba, and Fieldglass. Key idea: SaaS only—no table names like in ERP; you work with entities (User, Employee, Position) and UIs/APIs.

SAP SuccessFactors covers the full employee lifecycle in the cloud. Data lives in SAP-operated tenants and is accessed via web UIs and APIs. Integration with S/4HANA and other cloud products is common.

Purpose and scope

SuccessFactors addresses talent acquisition (Recruiting), new-hire experience (Onboarding), core HR and org structure (Employee Central), goal and performance management (Performance & Goals), compensation planning (EC Compensation), learning and development (Learning Management), and succession planning. Workforce Analytics provides reporting and people insights. Employee Central can replace or complement on-premise PA/OM; payroll can stay on-premise (S/4HANA) or use SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central Payroll (ECP) or third-party payroll with replication from EC.

Features in detail

  • Recruiting (RMK): Job requisitions, candidate application, screening, interviews, offers. Integrates with onboarding.
  • Onboarding: New-hire tasks, documents, and workflows so new employees are productive quickly.
  • Employee Central (EC): Core HR in the cloud: organization structure (org units, positions, jobs), employee profile (personal, employment, compensation), position management. Foundation for other modules (goals, performance, comp).
  • Goals (PM-GM) and Performance (PM): Set goals; performance review cycles; 360 feedback; calibration. Tied to compensation and development.
  • Compensation (EC Comp): Merit and bonus planning; budget allocation; approval workflows.
  • Learning (LMS): Learning assignments, courses, certifications; compliance training; integration with content providers.
  • Succession: Talent pools, succession plans, readiness for key positions.

Data plane and control plane

Data plane: No customer-accessible database. Data is stored as entities in the tenant (User, Employee, Position, Goal, Course, etc.). Access is via UIs (SuccessFactors apps) and APIs (OData, compound employee API). Replication and integration push/pull data to S/4HANA, payroll, or BI. Control plane: Admin Center (provisioning, company settings, picklists, integration center); RBP (rule-based permissions) for who sees what; IAS (SAP Identity Authentication Service) for SSO and identity; Integration Center for inbound/outbound mappings. See Module 1.

Deployment and integration

Deployment: SaaS only. Customer gets a tenant (e.g. one per company or per region). Region (data center) is chosen at provisioning. Integration: S/4HANA (employee, cost center, org for payroll and FI); payroll (ECP or third-party via CPI/file); Concur (expenses); Ariba/Fieldglass where used; IAS for SSO; CPI and Integration Center for sync and APIs (Module 29).

Example: New hire from Recruiting to Employee Central

A hiring manager creates a requisition; a candidate is hired and appears in Employee Central.

  1. Requisition (Recruiting): Manager creates job req for “Software Engineer” in department X. Candidates apply; interviews and offer are managed in Recruiting. Offer is accepted.
  2. Onboarding: New hire is triggered. Onboarding tasks: complete I-9, sign policies, assign equipment. Optional integration creates the employee in EC when offer is accepted or on start date.
  3. Employee Central: Employee record is created: personal data, org assignment (position, cost center), employment details. This data can be replicated to S/4HANA (cost center for cost allocation) or to payroll. Goals and learning can be assigned from EC data.

Result: Single employee record in the cloud; S/4HANA and payroll stay in sync via integration.

Key concepts and admin

Admin Center: Company settings, foundation objects (picklists, country, currency), integration center (inbound/outbound), provisioning. RBP (rule-based permissions): Defines who can view/edit which employees and fields (e.g. by org, position, location). Integration Center: Mappings for replication to/from S/4HANA, payroll, and other systems. IAS: Central identity and SSO for SAP cloud apps. EC vs on-premise HCM: EC is the cloud replacement for PA/OM; many customers run EC + on-premise or ECP payroll.
