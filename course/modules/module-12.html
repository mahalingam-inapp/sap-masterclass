Module 12: Warehouse Management / EWM

For beginners: Warehouse Management (WM) and Extended Warehouse Management (EWM) handle where stock sits inside the warehouse and how it is moved. When goods arrive (MM goods receipt), putaway moves them from the receiving area to a storage bin (a specific location). When a delivery is due (SD), picking moves the required quantity from the bin to the outbound area. These moves are done via transfer orders (or in EWM, warehouse tasks). WM is the classic solution; EWM adds waves (group many picks together), labor management, and yard management. In S/4HANA, embedded EWM runs in the same system as MM/SD. Key terms: storage type, storage bin, quant (quantity in a bin), transfer order.

SAP WM and EWM cover warehouse operations: putaway, picking, transfer orders, and physical inventory. Both are triggered by MM (goods movements) and SD (deliveries).

Purpose and scope

WM/EWM ensures that stock is stored in the right location (storage type, bin) and that outbound processes (picking, packing) are executed correctly. Key concepts: Warehouse number (logical warehouse); storage type (e.g. fixed bin, bulk, picking zone); storage bin (LAGP in WM); quant (quantity of a material in a bin, LQUA); transfer order (move from bin A to bin B, or for putaway/picking). EWM adds: wave (grouping outbound deliveries for picking), labor management (tasks and productivity), yard management (inbound/outbound trucks), and integration with Transportation Management (TM).

In plain terms: In simple WM, you have a warehouse number, storage types (e.g. “fixed bin” vs “bulk”), and bins (e.g. A-01-01). A quant is “100 PC of material X in bin A-01-01.” A transfer order says “move 50 PC from A-01-01 to PICK-01”; when the warehouse worker confirms it, the system updates the bin quantities. EWM’s wave groups several outbound deliveries so that one picking run can fulfill many orders at once, which is more efficient.

Features in detail

  • Putaway: After goods receipt (MM), WM/EWM creates putaway task or transfer order: move from GR zone (or staging) to storage bin. Can be system-directed (best bin) or manual. Confirmation updates bin stock.
  • Picking: Outbound delivery (SD) or production order (PP) creates pick request. WM/EWM creates transfer order: pick from bin to staging/packing. After confirmation, delivery can be processed (goods issue).
  • Transfer order (WM): LT01 (create), LT0A (create for delivery). Document that moves quantity from source to destination bin. Confirmation (LT12 or mobile) updates LQUA.
  • EWM wave: Group multiple outbound deliveries into a wave; create warehouse tasks (pick) for the wave. Optimizes picking routes and labor.
  • Physical inventory: Count at storage bin level; compare with system stock; post differences (MM adjustment).

Data plane and control plane

Data plane: WM: LAGP (storage bin), LQUA (quant), transfer order tables; delivery (LIKP, LIPS) and material document (MSEG) link. EWM: /SCWM/* tables (product, warehouse order, task). Control plane: Warehouse number, storage type and section, bin structure; putaway/picking strategies; EWM customizing (SPRO). See Module 1.

Deployment and integration

Deployment: On‑premise and S/4HANA. EWM can be embedded in S/4HANA (same system as MM/SD) or standalone (separate EWM system, integration via RFC/IDoc). Integration: Triggered by MM (goods receipt, transfer) and SD (outbound delivery); stock and quantity sync with MM. TM (Module 18) for freight when EWM and TM are used together.

Example: Goods receipt and putaway, then outbound pick

Plant 1000 uses embedded EWM. Warehouse 001.

  1. Goods receipt (MIGO): Post GR for 100 PC material M-001. MM updates stock (unrestricted). EWM receives the GR: creates putaway warehouse task (or transfer order in WM): move from GR zone to storage bin (e.g. 01-01-01-A1). Warehouse worker confirms putaway. Stock in bin 01-01-01-A1: 100 PC M-001.
  2. Outbound delivery (SD): Sales order created; delivery 8000000789 created (VL01N). Delivery requires 20 PC M-001. EWM creates pick task: pick 20 from 01-01-01-A1 to staging. Worker confirms pick. Staging has 20 PC for the delivery.
  3. Goods issue (SD): Post goods issue for delivery. MM reduces stock; delivery completed. Remaining bin stock: 80 PC M-001.

Result: Stock always has a bin location; putaway and pick are documented and confirmed.

Important T-codes and concepts

WM: LT01 (create transfer order), LT0A (TO for delivery), LT12 (confirm). EWM: /SCWM/… (Fiori apps and transactions for EWM). Concepts: Warehouse number; storage type and bin; transfer order; putaway vs picking strategy; embedded vs standalone EWM.

📝 Module Quiz

Q1: EWM can be:

  • A) Only on-premise classic
  • B) Only Ariba
  • C) Only Concur
  • D) Embedded in S/4HANA or standalone
Correct: D
EWM runs embedded in S/4HANA or as standalone.

Q2: Transfer order is used for:

  • A) Sales order only
  • B) Putaway and picking in warehouse
  • C) Only FI posting
  • D) Only CO
Correct: B
Transfer order moves stock within warehouse (putaway, picking).

Q3: WM/EWM is triggered by:

  • A) Only FI
  • B) Only CO
  • C) MM (goods movement) and SD (delivery)
  • D) Only QM
Correct: C
MM movements and SD deliveries trigger warehouse activities.

Q4: What does “quant” (LQUA) represent?

  • A) A sales order
  • B) A quantity of a material in a storage bin
  • C) A purchase order
  • D) A G/L account
Correct: B
Quant = one batch/quantity of material in one bin; LQUA stores it in WM.
